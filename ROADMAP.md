# Roadmap

> Last updated: 2026-06-01. This roadmap is directional, not a commitment.

## Planned

- **Graph grounding** — ground conversations in a knowledge graph so Discopilot can answer questions with verifiable, contextual references.
- **Real-time translation** — translate voice across languages live so multilingual servers can converse without friction.
- **Intelligent recap** — auto-generate concise summaries of voice sessions, highlighting decisions, action items, and key moments.
- **Suggested replies** — surface context-aware reply suggestions in text and voice channels to speed up responses.
- **Live meeting captions and transcript** — stream low-latency captions during voice sessions and persist a searchable transcript afterwards.
- **Ask any question about the meeting** — query the meeting in natural language while it's in progress or after it ends, with answers grounded in the live transcript.
